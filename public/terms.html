What FORENSIQ is

FORENSIQ is an automated forensics scanner for tokens on Robinhood Chain. When you submit a contract or pair address, our backend runs a series of automated checks — market data analysis, contract and holder analysis, GitHub repository analysis, website analysis, and social media analysis — and returns a rule-engine and AI-generated report. The report includes a tier verdict, a confidence score, and a list of findings.
